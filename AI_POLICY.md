# AI Contributions Policy

Some of this project's code and prose was created, in part or in whole, by generative AI tools. These tools carry real risks, and I take responsibility for using them safely and effectively in my own contributions.

I welcome external contributions. I ask only that you apply the same restraint I do, both for my sake and for the community's.

In short, this means:

- Understand the code you submit. You are responsible for your tool's output, and no one else is.
- Do not introduce ["license laundering"](https://crashoverride.com/resources/knowledge-base/ai-code-governance/ai-coding-license-risk) or similar copyright violations through unreviewed or unaudited code.
  - This matters most for the catalog, which is licensed under CC0.
- Ground your tool in the actual mechanics of the game, to avoid hallucinations.
- Take additional care with AI-generated prose that end users will see.
- Do not add `Co-authored-by` trailers that credit the tool you used. I do not give free advertising, and you shouldn't either. Guides online explain how to remove them.
